# Business Rules

## Known Rules

No business rules have been formally provided for this proof of concept.

## Testing Guidance

The QA system must not invent business rules.

When behavior is observed during exploration, classify it as:

* Observed behavior
* Expected behavior based on explicit requirements
* Assumption
* Potential defect

If the expected behavior cannot be established from requirements or available product knowledge, report the uncertainty rather than presenting the behavior as a confirmed defect.
